Use the normal 1× lens, not ultra-wide. Small wobbles are fine. If the phone gets knocked, draw the wicket again.

Setup

  1. Tap Draw wicket and draw a box around the stumps with your finger, from the bails down to the ground.
  2. Optional: tap Wide markers, then tap the wide line on each side of the pitch.
  3. Bowl. Only balls coming towards the stumps are judged.

Distance is estimated from the stumps' height in the picture. If you measure the real distance with a tape, enter it once and the estimate locks to your phone's lens.
