Use a stand or tripod. The screen stays on while the camera runs. Small wobbles are fine, and the wicket follows small shifts of the phone by itself; if it gets knocked hard, draw the wicket again. Zoom with + / − on the picture or by pinching; about 2× helps the far wicket and ball show up bigger.

Setup

  1. Put the phone behind the bowler's stumps (the umpire's spot), looking at the batter's stumps.
  2. Tap Set up wicket. A frame appears: move it onto the far stumps with the arrows (or drag anywhere on the picture) and make it fit with − / +.
  3. The wide lines are placed for you. To fine-tune, tap Wide lines and use the same buttons. Tap Done.
  4. Bowl. Only balls travelling towards the batter's wicket are judged.

The far stumps are a pitch length (20.12 m) plus this distance away.
