LBW calls, wides and ball speed from a phone at the bowler's end. Plus a simple match scorer.

  1. Set the phone up on a stand behind the bowler's stumps, looking at the batter's stumps.
  2. Line up the wicket with the arrows and − / +. The wide lines and pitch markings follow.
  3. Pick RH or LH for the batter, lock the screen, and bowl.

Camera & distance

The far stumps are a pitch length (20.12 m) plus this distance. Use a stand or tripod; the screen stays on while the camera runs. The wicket follows small shifts of the phone by itself. Zooming to about 2× makes the far wicket and ball show up bigger.
