Set up behind the stumps

Fix your phone 2 to 4 metres behind the stumps, about stump height, looking down the pitch. Then start the camera or open a clip.

Use the normal 1× lens, not ultra-wide. The phone must not move once the wicket is placed.

Wicket

  1. Tap Place wicket.
  2. Press at the bottom of the middle stump and drag up to the top of the stumps.
  3. Drag the virtual wicket to fine-tune it.
  4. Bowl.

Distance is estimated from the stumps' height in the picture. If you measure the real distance with a tape, enter it once and the estimate locks to your phone's lens.
